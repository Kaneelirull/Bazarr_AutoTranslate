PROJECT PLAN - Canonicalize embedded translation outputs
LAST UPDATED: 2026-08-20

GOAL:
Publish embedded-track translations as Bazarr-recognizable `<video>.<language>[.hi|.sdh].srt` files, safely recover submission-backed orphan outputs, and make completed-without-output retries durable.

BRANCH:
- `fix/canonicalize-embedded-translation-output`, created from freshly fetched `origin/main`.
- User-owned untracked subtitle fixtures under `examples/MissingRepair/` remain excluded.

IMPLEMENTED:
- Numeric extraction collision variants are removed from canonical targets; semantic HI and SDH variants remain.
- Both Lingarr layouts are watched: `extracted.<target>.<variant>.srt` and `extracted.<variant>.<target>.srt`.
- Changed provider candidates are atomically published to the canonical path before provenance and validation.
- Pre-submission recovery requires an exact candidate, a matching non-terminal attempt, the current source path/hash, and an output timestamp at or after submission.
- Receipt-owned, stale, unmatched, and wrong-source extracted files remain untouched; canonical publication never overwrites a concurrent file.
- Completed jobs with no output atomically fail their attempt and create/update a `whole_file` retry plan under `completed_output_missing`. A failed transaction retains the original submission cooldown and reports degraded state.
